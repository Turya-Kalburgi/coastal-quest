# Coastal Quest 🌊🛹

*A cozy pixel-art exploration game hidden behind an Instagram bio.*

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Vision

Instead of opening a normal bio link, visitors enter a peaceful
pixel-art world.

There are: - No enemies - No timers - No levels - No combat - No
pressure

The goal is to slow down, explore, discover hidden objects, and enjoy
the atmosphere.

Every adventure feels different because quests and collectible locations
change.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Gameplay

## Starting the Adventure

The player spawns on a beautiful coastal road during golden hour.

The character starts riding a skateboard.

The camera follows from a cozy top-down perspective.

Ocean waves move naturally beside the road.

Relaxing music starts immediately.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Skateboarding

The skateboard can only be used on roads and paved paths.

When the player reaches:

-   Sand
-   Beach
-   Grass
-   Forest
-   Wooden docks
-   Cliffs

The character automatically:

1.  Steps off the skateboard.
2.  Picks it up with one hand.
3.  Walks while carrying it.

Returning to the road automatically places the skateboard down and
resumes skating.

# World

-   Beach
-   Ocean shoreline
-   Palm trees
-   Forest paths
-   Lighthouse
-   Wooden dock
-   Hidden caves
-   Secret viewpoints
-   Clifftops
-   Campsite

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Quest System

Every new adventure randomly selects **4--5 collectibles** from a larger
pool.

Example:

Today's Finds

☐ Camera ☐ Coffee Cup ☐ Headphones ☐ Seashell ☐ Message in a Bottle

Each session has different objectives and different item locations.

# Ending

When every collectible is found:

The player walks to a scenic ocean viewpoint.

The camera slowly zooms out.

Music becomes softer.

Ocean waves continue moving.

A random quote fades in.

Examples:

-   Nice afternoon.
-   Found a few things today.
-   Somewhere worth coming back to.
-   The ocean keeps its secrets.
-   The tide will be different tomorrow.
-   See you on the next adventure.

Button:

Generate New Adventure

This creates:

-   New random quest
-   New collectible locations
-   Fresh adventure

# Design Philosophy

This is not a game about winning.

It is a tiny world hidden behind an Instagram bio where visitors can
slow down, explore a beautiful coastline, collect a few memorable
objects, enjoy peaceful music, and leave with a smile.
